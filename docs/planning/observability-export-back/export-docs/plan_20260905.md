## 1 · Design decisions (settled — do not re-litigate mid-build)

1. **Scope discipline is the whole risk.** A line that says "export-back deferred" is
   retired **only** if the shipped slice achieves what the line refers to. The slice
   ships: verdicts → OTLP/JSON span attributes/events → a fixture collector (file/stdout).
   It does **not** ship: a live OTLP exporter, anything Langfuse-specific, or
   multi-trace aggregation. Every surviving "deferred"/"not built" claim names exactly
   what remains deferred.
2. **The "no Langfuse integration" lines survive verbatim.** `ROADMAP.md:263`'s
   prohibition ("Do not stage a Langfuse screenshot…") is untouched; only its
   "exporting verdicts back into a collector is a named deferral" clause updates.
3. **Launch assets are owner territory.** `docs/planning/launch-demo/ph-assets.md` and
   the launch-demo PRD lines are **not** edited by this unit (three questions are open
   for the owner there, `CHECKLIST.md:330-334`). The L7 checklist box is untouched
   (owner-ticked by pre-registration). The "READY TO PUBLISH" gate is untouched
   (export-back is not a publish condition).
4. **The stale `NOT_COVERED` deferral item is corrected.** `CAPABILITY_ROADMAP.md:860-861`
   lists "the `NOT_COVERED` reclassification" as deferred to the second aspect; it
   shipped via `interop-merge-repair` (`STATUS.md:660-673`). The correction is a
   correction, not a reclassification: no published number, status, or verdict moves.
5. **No published number is re-derived or restated** (`11/60`, `precision 0.00`, `1/15`,
   `4/16` — not touched, not re-quoted unless already present and unchanged).
