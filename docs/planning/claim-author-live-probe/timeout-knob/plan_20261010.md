## Testing strategy

All deterministic, no network, no real model: small-val sleep stubs only (sleep 0.5 s,
timeout 2.0 s family), no timing assertions on elapsed wall (the repo's standing rule for
timeout tests, `test_verify_author.py:83-87` precedent). The e2e sleep-stub at the
SHIPPED 60 s cap runs only in the darwin e2e module and is NOT modified (its
"no-reply-within-{timeout}" detail byte-identity must hold at a non-default resolved
bound via a new small stub, not a rewrite of `sleep.py`).

## Environment & determinism

`BELAY_CLAIM_AUTHOR_TIMEOUT` (seconds, float); no clocks in assertions; everything
committed is byte-reproducible.

## Edge cases

Blank/≤0/un-lexable env ⇒ 60.0; flag beats env; unknown flag on `phase0 run` remains
exit 2 (surface untouched); a timeout at the resolved bound yields byte-identical
`AUTHOR_TIMED_OUT`/`NO_CHECK_AUTHOR` — never a crash, never a hang.
