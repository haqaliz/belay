# Implementation plan — `coverage-surface-parity` (aspect 2 of 3)

**Date:** 2026-09-21 · **Branch:** `feat/effect-conformance-coverage/aliz`
**Spec:** `docs/planning/effect-conformance-coverage/coverage-surface-parity/spec.md`
**Depends on:** aspect 1 (`absent-contract-coverage`), landed in 6 commits `b80dd0d`..`09928f5`.

**Why this aspect is not optional.** Aspect 1 made a `NOT_COVERED` dimension **routine** — every
turn against an annotation-less server now carries one. Four surfaces render a clean/PASS status
with **no coverage disclosure and no coverage test**, so aspect 1 alone ships a PASS that can
travel silently. The precedent's hard rule
(`verdict-coverage-status/prd.md:203-209`):

> **no surface may render a turn's status without also rendering its coverage line — enforced by
> a test per surface, not by review.**

**Do not merge or release between aspect 1 and this aspect.**

---

## Phase 5 — `interop export` + the name collision (spec items 4, 5)

- **`src/belay/interop/export.py:109`** — `_coverage_dimensions` writes `belay.verdict.coverage`
  only `if uncovered_kinds:` and carries **kinds without the message**. Decide and pin one:
  always write it (absent-never-zero, matching `verify --json`), or document the conditional.
  Prefer **always write** — it matches the shipped rule elsewhere that the coverage key is
  present-but-empty rather than absent.
- **The `coverage` word is overloaded three ways** in the CLI: this coverage boundary;
  `corpus score`'s adjudicable-label metric (`cli.py:2196`); `belay replay`'s
  replayed/unverified counts (`cli.py:477`). Renaming is out of scope; **misleading a reader is
  not acceptable either** — disambiguate in help text.
- **Validation:** `uv run pytest tests/test_interop_export.py tests/test_coverage_surface_parity.py -q`
- **Commit:** `feat(coverage-parity): always emit the OTLP coverage attribute; disambiguate the overloaded coverage label`
