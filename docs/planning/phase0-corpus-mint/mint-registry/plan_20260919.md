## 2. The decision this aspect must get right

### 2.1 The exclusion set is DERIVED, never transcribed

`observed.json` holds 23 ids and is **stale** — the gate mint drove ~60 more, recoverable
from the committed ledgers (64 distinct `trace_id`s across
`docs/planning/mint-shell-toolset-run/mint-run/ledgers/`). A hand-copied id list is the
defect class that broke `test_docker_inimage.py`'s dev-dep list: two lists and nothing
connecting them.

So the generator computes, at generation time:

```
excluded = every real instance_id appearing in ANY committed eval/instances/*.json
           (except pool.json itself)
fresh    = pool.json reals - excluded        # the measured 30
```

**Registry membership, not ledger membership** — and §2.2 says why that conservatism is
required rather than merely cautious.

### 2.2 Why conservative (30), not derived (83) — a contract, not caution

| Measure | Fresh | Counts |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative — minus every registry's reals | **30** (django 23, sympy 7) | never *drawn* |
| Derived — minus `observed.json` ∪ all 12 ledgers | **83** (django 53, sympy 30) | never *captured* |

The gap is attrition: drawn into `s6stage3.json`, never captured. Some were **attempted
and failed**, and a failed attempt **is an observation** — *"an instance that produced an
observation is never re-armable"* (`checkpoint.py:18-21`). Separating attempted-from-
never-reached needs the s6 **checkpoint**, and **measured: no s6 checkpoint survives**
(`~/dev/at/holder/belay/mint/` holds only `s1 s1b s1p s2 s3 live-smoke-claude-cli`).

So the 83 hides an **unidentifiable** subset that must not be re-driven. The 30 is
provably safe and, at n≈12, ample.

### 2.3 The monoculture is stated, never simulated

Both measures are ~100% django+sympy. `selection.py:3-24` exists because *"a uniform draw
of 50 would publish a django/sympy rate as an agent rate"*; the residue is worse than the
concentration the draw was built to correct — the small-repo block was exhausted by
stage3's draw, *"takes every one of them"* (`build_gate_registries.py` docstring).

**Do not call `select_instances` to simulate stratification that no longer exists.** With
a single-repo-family pool the rebalance is a no-op dressed as rigor. Draw deterministically
from the fresh set under a recorded seed and **state the composition in the header**.

This is the mechanical reason Q1 publishes **no violation rate**.

## 3. Stage structure (Rule A, sized for a non-gate run)

| Stage | Records | Gate before the next |
|---|---|---|
| **cm-stage1** (probe) | CTL-1 + CTL-4 = **2** | capture produced AND ≥1 verifiable turn AND both controls clean — else **STOP**: instrument or wiring defect, before spending |
| **cm-stage2** | CTL-2 + CTL-3 + **8 fresh real** = **10** | controls first; a control FAIL **VOIDS** the run (D-3) |

Total real instances: **8**, plus 4 control drives. **Stage 3 is not attempted** and the
header says so — the ≥50 denominator is out of reach safely (§2.2) and out of scope (Q1).

CTL-4 (`control__flask-verify-with-command`) is the **positive** control and only produces
evidence under `--toolset filesystem+shell` — it gives the trajectory axis its PASS side,
the gap named as caveat (4) on the 18.3% result.

## 5. Where the agent must STOP and ask

1. **The fresh set is not 30.** The measurement is pinned in the PRD; a different number
   means the exclusion rule drifted — report, do not adjust the target.
2. **Any temptation to widen eligibility** to reach a bigger n. The filters are pinned;
   *record a discrepancy, never tune the rule*.
3. **Any temptation to call `select_instances`** for stratification (§2.3).
4. **A control id collides with a banked corpus case.** Fail-closed; report.

## 6. Out of scope

- Driving the mint (aspect `mint-run`).
- Re-generating `observed.json` — tempting, and a genuine follow-up, but it changes a
  committed artifact other registries were built from. Not in a unit whose rule is that no
  published number moves.
- Widening `pool.json`.
