A pre-unit ledger or corpus case never has `sub_cause` → renders exactly as today. A
sub-cause present on a non-`NO_CHECK_AUTHOR` record (hand-edited) → rendered as stored,
never inferred; the corpus loader does not reject it (validation is type-only).
